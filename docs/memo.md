# Priya Raman — Q3 training-review memo

## Executive finding

Observed CSAT is 3.33/5 across 5,196 survey responses (44.2% response rate) on 11,750 tickets. The trend declined from 3.60 in October 2025 to 3.29 in December 2025, then was 3.40 in June 2026. The dashboard identifies ten Tier 1 training-review candidates using reviewable evidence rather than calling them the “worst” employees: each meets a data-derived threshold of 93 rated tickets and is compared with the relevant Tier + team peer baseline.

## Business context

### Measurable Q3 operating target

Reduce the first-response breach rate from 9.72% in April-June 2026 to a 7.0% management target in the next comparable quarter. At the same 2,067-ticket volume, this means approximately 56 fewer policy-mandated ₹350 SLA credits, or ₹19,700 per quarter. This is a management scenario for monitoring, not a promise that training causes the result.

The Q3 training budget is ₹4,00,000. The dashboard does not allocate it automatically. It provides a transparent starting set of ten evidence-backed reviews. In the supplied period, 1,896 replacements have a policy planning cost of ₹34,15,990 (unit cost + ₹340 logistics each) and 1,064 breached first-response targets carry ₹3,72,400 in separate SLA credits. These are distinct cost lines, not a combined savings claim.

## Training themes and caveats

The largest supplied issue categories are Delivery & Shipping (1,884 tickets), Other (1,732), Billing & Payments (1,471), Charging & Battery (1,355), and Returns & Refunds (1,116). Candidate-specific category and operational indicators should guide the review conversation. Tier 2 Escalations & Warranty is deliberately not ranked with Tier 1; its cases are certified and multi-touch. CSAT is respondent-only, queue mix can confound scores, legacy resolution timestamps are normalized to IST, and 14 clearly unusable IVR fragments are excluded from optional AI sampling.

## Next action

Use the ten entries as a manager-led review queue: inspect de-identified ticket samples, validate category-level themes, agree coaching goals, and monitor the same peer-aware metrics after training. Do not make compensation or disciplinary decisions from this dashboard alone.
